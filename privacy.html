Privacy Policy

Last updated July 5, 2026

This privacy policy covers senylabs.io, the website of Seny Labs LLC. Our mobile applications have their own privacy policies, linked from each product.

What we collect

If you join a product waitlist, we collect your email address. That is the only personal information this website collects. We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking technologies of any kind.

How we use it

Your email is used for one purpose: notifying you when the product you signed up for launches. We do not sell, share, or disclose your email to third parties. Emails are stored securely with our infrastructure provider, Supabase.

Your rights

You can request access to or deletion of your email at any time by contacting info@senylabs.io. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.
