Privacy Policy

01Scope

This privacy policy covers senylabs.io, the website of Seny Labs LLC. Our applications have their own privacy policies, linked from each product.

02What we collect

If you sign up for launch notes, we collect your email address. If you contact us through the form on our Work with us page, we collect the name, email, company, and project details you enter. That is all the personal information this website collects. We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking technologies of any kind.

03How we use it

Launch-note emails are used for one purpose: telling you when we release something. Project inquiries are used only to reply to you. We do not sell, share, or disclose this information to third parties. It is stored securely with our infrastructure provider, Supabase.

04Your rights

You can request access to or deletion of your information at any time using the contact details below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.
