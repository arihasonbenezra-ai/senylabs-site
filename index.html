02  /  How we build

4 rules
01

Small team, long leash.

Whoever builds a product owns it, from the first commit to launch.

02

AI in the tooling and the product.

We use it to build faster, and we build it into what we ship: memory, adaptation, fewer repeated decisions.

03

One job per app.

Each app does one thing for one part of your week. We ship, watch how people use it, and remove what they don't.

04

Designed for the moment of use.

A match kicking off. An interview in ten minutes. Whatever we build has to work right then, with no setup and nothing to learn.
